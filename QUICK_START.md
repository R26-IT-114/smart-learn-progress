## ⚡ Quick Customization (5 Minutes)

### **1. Update Institution**
Open `src/App.js`, find "SLIIT" and replace with your institution name.

### **2. Add Supervisor**
In Contact Section, fill in:
- Supervisor name
- Email address
- Contact number

### **3. Update GitHub Link**
Replace placeholder with your actual GitHub repository URL.

### **4. Add Team Members**
In Team Section, update the 4 team members with their names and roles.

### **5. Update Project Email**
In Contact Section, add your project email.
